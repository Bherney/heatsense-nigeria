# Changelog

## v2.0 (October 2026)

- Climate zones are now the main unit of analysis; the spatial lens offers Grid or Climate zone, with states as a boundary overlay.
- Replaced 2050 projections with a weekly heatwave outlook for lead weeks 1 to 4: chance of a heatwave, expected heatwave days, duration, peak intensity and prediction confidence.
- New Outlook and skill tab: outlook by climate zone, forecast skill against climatology for five approaches, and actions for NiMet, NCDC, NEMA, the Nigerian Red Cross Society and state ministries of health at each warning level.
- New City view (1 km) tab: the outlook downscaled to about 1 km over 17 cities with bilinear interpolation.
- Selection panel shows a four-week outlook strip for any cell, zone or state.
- Ask HeatSense answers questions about the coming weeks and about how far the outlook can be trusted.
- The "Dual-lens comparison" tab is now "Zones and states".

## v1.0

- First release: observed heatwave record 2003 to 2025 (frequency, duration, intensity), trends, hotspots, heat regimes, vulnerability surface with driver attribution, state ranking, location lookup and the Ask HeatSense query panel.
