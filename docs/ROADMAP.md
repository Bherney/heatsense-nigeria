# Roadmap

## Completed

| Phase | Work | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | MSc research: space-time pattern mining of Sentinel-5P air pollutants and MODIS land surface temperature across all 37 Nigerian states (72 months) | Published in *Theoretical and Applied Climatology* (2026); 2026 Esri Young Scholar Award for Nigeria; best MSc dissertation in the Department of Geography, University of Ibadan |
| National pilot | Heatwave characterisation and hybrid explainable machine learning vulnerability modelling across the six climate zones | Manuscript under review at *Theoretical and Applied Climatology* |
| Urban heat | Surface urban heat island of Ibadan from Landsat 8/9, Getis-Ord Gi* hot-spot clustering, LST and vegetation relationships | Manuscript under review at *Discover Geoscience* |
| Demonstrator v1 | Interactive web demonstrator: observed record, vulnerability, drivers, plain-English questions | Released |
| Demonstrator v2 (October 2026) | Climate zones as the main unit; weekly outlook for lead weeks 1 to 4; forecast skill comparison; agency action table; 1 km city view; outlook-aware Ask HeatSense | Live at https://heatsense.netlify.app/ |

## Doctoral research plan (36 months)

| Months | Work | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 6 | Literature synthesis, Tier 1 ingestion pipeline, first advisory panel meeting, partner requirements workshop | Ingestion pipeline operational; interface specification agreed |
| 7 to 14 | Heatwave characterisation, trends, open-source space-time cube implementation | Package released; Paper 1 submitted |
| 15 to 22 | Vulnerability modelling, SHAP attribution, spatial regression, mid-project validation workshop | Model validated under spatial cross-validation; Paper 2 submitted |
| 23 to 30 | Fine-scale prediction model, testing with partners, Tier 4 assistant | Weekly outlook tested; Papers 3 and 4 drafted |
| 31 to 36 | Warning thresholds with partners, training and hand-over, thesis writing | System transferred; thesis submitted |

## Planned outputs

- Four peer-reviewed papers: national heatwave characterisation; explainable vulnerability modelling; fine-scale heatwave prediction and its skill; the open agentic system.
- An open-source Python package for space-time pattern mining.
- A public web map service and the HeatSense application.
- Plain-language briefs and training for partner agencies.
- A handed-over system that Nigerian agencies run themselves.

## Next releases of this demonstrator

- v2.1: load pre-computed layers from external JSON so real results can replace synthetic ones layer by layer.
- v2.2: add state and local government area boundary overlays at finer detail.
- v3.0: connect the Ask HeatSense panel to the Tier 4 service.
